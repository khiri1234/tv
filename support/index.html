Frequently asked questions

Does H TV include any channels?

No. H TV is a player only. It doesn't provide or include any channels, movies or series. You add a playlist you already have from your provider.

How do I add a playlist?

In the app, add a new playlist and enter either:

  • an M3U link from your provider, or
  • your Xtream Codes server address, username and password.
Why don't I see Movies or Series?

Movies and series come from Xtream Codes logins. M3U playlists usually include live channels only. If your provider offers Xtream Codes details, add those instead.

A channel won't play or keeps buffering.
  • Check the same channel works in another app or on another device. If not, the problem is with the provider's stream.
  • Check your internet connection, and try again in a few minutes.
  • Make sure your subscription with your provider is active and isn't being used on more devices than it allows.
My playlist won't load.
  • Double-check the link or login details for typos, including http vs https and the port number.
  • Ask your provider whether the server address has changed.
The program guide is empty.

The guide shows "now" and "next" information that your provider supplies. If your provider doesn't send guide data for a channel, there is nothing for H TV to show.

How do I remove a playlist or my data?

Delete the playlist in the app, or delete the app. All your playlists, favorites and history are stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Still stuck? Email Hatef@hatef.cc with your device (iPhone, iPad, Apple TV or Mac), the app version, and what happens. Please don't send your playlist password.
