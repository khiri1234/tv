H TV Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

H TV ("the app") is a media player for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We have no servers that receive your data.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your own device: the playlist links (M3U URLs) or Xtream Codes server details and login you enter, your favorites and recently watched items, and cached channel and guide data. This information never leaves your device except as described below, and it is removed when you delete the playlist or the app.

Connections to your playlist provider

When you add a playlist, the app connects directly from your device to the server address you provide in order to download your channel list, program guide and video streams. Those requests go to your provider, not to us. Your provider's own privacy policy applies to that connection.

Content

H TV is a player only. It does not provide, host, or include any channels, streams or other content. You are responsible for using only playlists and content you have the legal right to access.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
